# Sources and confidence (v2, 2026-11-04)

Ideas only; no text copied. Label: P = platform statement, R = research, V = vendor data, B = creator/blog opinion.

## Read in full (14 pages)
- Muslim Creator School landing page (B, seller): says format, topic, first 3 s, on-screen content, story order, closing ask and cut work together; one reel went 1,507 -> 516,056 views, watch time 9 s -> 11 s and 14 s. Self-reported, no method; the course is paid and unread.
- Adam Connell, video retention (B): open loop, visual pattern interrupt, seamless loop ending.
- OutlierKit, hooks and retention (B): promise, keep it, next promise; early drop = hook problem, slow slide = retention problem.
- Social Media Today, Mosseri's algorithm video (P, quoting his Instagram post): top signals watch time, likes per reach, sends per reach; likes slightly more for connected reach, sends for unconnected.
- Metricool and Social Samosa on Instagram's Retention chart and Skip Rate (P, reported): skip rate = views skipped in first 3 s; replaces view rate; retention chart by second.
- TrueFuture Media, lowering skip rate (B): one promise per reel, show the result or problem before background, compare with your own median.
- Async/Podcastle, reel drop-off (B): fixes by cause; test the middle by cutting 20-30%.
- vidIQ, Shorts algorithm (B/secondary): since 31 Mar 2025 every start and loop is a view; Engaged Views exclude loops.
- Creator Essentials glossary, Viewed vs Swiped Away (secondary): no universal benchmark; reasons viewers stay (recognisable subject, relevant question, early result or conflict, movement in the first frame, clear audio and captions).
- Bytecap, Shorts retention benchmarks (B): benchmarks depend on length, topic, audience; use channel-relative.
- Wistia, video CTAs (V): 36,000+ CTAs; best position by length (under 1 min: first quarter; 1-3 min: last quarter; 3-5 min: middle; 5-30 min: end). Hosted marketing video with a clickable CTA; Wistia sells the feature.
- CapCut, 60-second story arc (B/vendor): six beats (hook, context, conflict, escalation, payoff, action); 3-7-21 test; one promise, one visible change, one payoff.
- Virlo, story gap (B): cliffhanger/curiosity-gap framing.
