# Audio

Sound effects and the music pad are not in this repo. They came from Mixkit (free licence,
not for redistribution as files). Download your own and put them in `public/audio/`
inside a working copy of a template. File names the templates expect:

air-woosh-quick.mp3, chime-crystal.mp3, pad.mp3 (quiet ambient, 25s or longer),
switch-tap.mp3, swoosh-slow.mp3, transition-soft.mp3, ui-message-pop.mp3,
ui-notify-tech.mp3, ui-option-select.mp3, ui-select-click.mp3, ui-success-soft.mp3
